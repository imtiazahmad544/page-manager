Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Page Manager application ("the App") handles information. The App is a private tool operated by Imtiaz Ahmad ("the Operator") for the sole purpose of publishing and scheduling video content to Facebook Pages that the Operator owns and administers.

1. Who uses this App

The App is not a public product. It is used only by the Operator, through the Operator's own Facebook account, to manage the Operator's own Facebook Pages. It does not offer accounts, sign-ups, or services to any other person.

2. Information the App accesses

Through the Facebook Graph API, and only with the Operator's own authorization, the App accesses:

  • The list of Facebook Pages administered by the Operator (names and Page IDs).
  • Page access tokens for those Pages, used to publish and schedule posts and Reels.
  • Basic profile information of the Operator (name and user ID) to identify the authorized account.

The App does not access information about any other Facebook user, and it does not read private messages, friends lists, or personal data of any third party.

3. How the information is used

The information above is used for one purpose only: uploading and scheduling the Operator's own video content to the Operator's own Facebook Pages, and recording which videos have been published. It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics about other people, or any form of data sale or sharing.

4. Storage

Access tokens are stored in the Operator's own private Google Drive storage and in the Operator's own secret-management settings for the publishing environment. No information is stored on third-party servers operated by anyone other than the Operator's own cloud accounts (Google Drive and Meta platforms).

5. Sharing

No information accessed by the App is sold, rented, or shared with any third party, except as transmitted to Meta's own APIs in order to publish content to the Operator's Pages.

6. Data deletion

Because the App processes only the Operator's own data, the Operator may delete all stored tokens and records at any time by removing them from the Operator's Google Drive and revoking the App's access in Facebook Settings → Business integrations. Any other person who believes the App has processed their data may contact the Operator at the address below and it will be deleted promptly.
